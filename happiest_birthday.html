My heart felt letter

My dearest baby love,

There are mornings I wake up and the world feels impossibly light and it is because of you. You have a way of making even my harshest and worst feel lighter, brighter and softer. I keep our moments tightly, securely and preciously between the pages of my memory, and I find myself returning to them whenever I need to feel at home and at peace.

You make my world such a better place to live in. A life and a world that I used to just survive in, has become something that I desire and wish to live in, together with you.

I do not know how to express the wonders you have made me feel and experience. I want to write to you a long love letter, but I think this simplicity and peace is the best and most bestest greatest way I can show you how happy and loved you have made me. Thank you so much for caring and holding me together for this past month.

I love you more than words could ever be able to carry.

Forever yours,  ♡
Matthew
